Proposed exact infinite-family theorem: for triangle0-1-2-0 with a pendant path of l edges at0, l>=0, clear=6*2^l-2. Lower is the prior April6.1 pure-pile obstruction (S005 Theorem19); upper is a new complete construction to be proved. This solves ASH*-clear on this family only, not stacking or universal ASH*. No novelty claim.

The constructive upper inducts by attaching one new leaf. At mass2g+2, even leaf piles contract directly, odd piles>=5 supply their own credit, while leaf1/3 use two pebbles at their neighbour. On the old triangle-tail, a rooted spanning broom oriented to its tip has height l+1: greedy inward moves produce two tip pebbles whenever mass>4*2^l, with each chosen output using <=2^(l+1) labelled initial pebbles. Thus the credit costs <=4*2^l<=g+1. After the two-move leaf debt cycle, at least g pebbles remain in the old core. Prove every inequality and legal schedule, including l=0, empty core sites, overlapping designated ancestries and arbitrary other piles. Do not treat this as necessity of greedy normalization or erase cyclic moves.
