## Overriding project goal

Aim directly at resolving universal critical Almost Stacked Hypothesis ASH*, rather than accumulating intermediate results.

For a finite connected simple graph G with n>=2, let stack(G) be the least t>=2 such that every configuration of exact mass t can be stacked. For connected non-bipartite G, define clear(G) analogously for reaching exactly one pebble. A legal move removes two pebbles from one vertex and places one at an adjacent vertex. A configuration is almost stacked if all but at most one vertex contain at most one pebble.

The universal target is:

- STACK: every eligible G has an almost-stacked non-stackable configuration of mass stack(G)-1.
- CLEAR: every eligible non-bipartite G has an almost-stacked non-clearable configuration of mass clear(G)-1.

Seek either an unconditional universal proof, with STACK and CLEAR resolved separately, or a fully certified mathematical counterexample to either assertion. A resolution of only one assertion must be reported with its exact scope.
