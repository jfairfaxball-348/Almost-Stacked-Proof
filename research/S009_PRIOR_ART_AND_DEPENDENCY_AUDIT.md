## Source slips reconciled without changing historical records

1. April Lemma7.1(1) p12 correctly pushes a legal sequence along a surjective graph homomorphism. Part(2) prints the threshold inequality in the opposite direction. The correct conclusion is g(G0)>=g(G1): lift each target-graph configuration by placing its mass at chosen preimages; if all source-graph configurations at mass t win, their pushed sequences make every target configuration win. April Cor7.5 p16 actually uses this correct lower direction. This is a source correction by elementary proof, not a claim that its authors have posted an erratum.
2. April Lemma9.2 p21's printed cost/bound is inconsistent, already at k2 with one endpoint singleton. October4.8 pp4–5 has the corrected exact cost. October4.9 likewise supplies the precise pushforward formula. Current debt proofs use October or explicitly proved S006 costs; the older April formulas are not dependencies.
3. April Prop8.4 pp17–18 repeatedly says 'clearable' during a proof whose bipartite conclusion is 'stackable'. Bipartite imbalance0 cannot clear. Read the induction as stacking, as its statement requires; do not infer a bipartite clearing theorem. Prop8.5's p20 step contains conflicting >=3 / =2 wording for the auxiliary heavy vertex. The needed case is >=2 in a third part when both chosen vertices are singletons; its three moves are legal even at2, and retained heavy/edge hypotheses must be checked on the output. We have inspected these source proofs, not assigned them a kernel assurance label.
4. Hurlbert's generalized family terminology has both 'every D' and weak 'some D'. The introduction's broad relationship is not an assertion that exact configuration equality and ordinary target domination are interchangeable.
